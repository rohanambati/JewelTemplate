Project brief for AI website builder

Goal

Build a production-ready jewelry e-commerce site named Terces Jewellery with elegant, luxurious styling and fast performance.

Brand and tone

Premium, modern, minimal.

Black/white base with gold accents.

Serif display headings + clean sans-serif body.

Structure and pages

Global: sticky top nav with logo Terces Jewellery left; links: Home, Collections, Buy, Offers, Support, Contact; right-side icons for search, account, cart. Responsive and keyboard accessible.

Home:

Hero: dark backdrop, spotlighted diamond necklace image, headline “Elevate Your Elegance”, subtext “Handcrafted jewelry where timeless design meets modern sophistication.”, CTA “Explore Collections”.

“Our Signature Collections” with 3 cards: Emerald Dreams, Timeless Diamonds, Golden Essence; each card: image, 1-sentence blurb, “View Collection”.

Product grid preview: 3 featured items with image, name, star rating, short copy, price, Add to Cart, wishlist heart. Grid/List toggle and Filters button.

Trust strip: “Free shipping over ₹5,000 – 30-day returns – Secure payments.”

Collections: masonry/grid with collection tiles (Emerald, Diamond, Gold, Sapphire, Pearls), each linking to product listings; short SEO text under each.

Buy (Shop): faceted filters (Category, Metal, Gemstone, Price range, Rating, Availability), Sort by (Popularity, Newest, Price, Rating), paginated product grid.

Offers: time-bound promotions with countdowns, promo badges, coupon entry.

Product detail: zoomable gallery, title, price, rating, stock, options (size/length/metal), quantity stepper, Add to Cart + Buy Now, materials/craftsmanship/warranty bullets, shipping/returns accordion, social share, related products carousel.

Support: FAQs, contact form (name/email/message), WhatsApp link, store policies.

Contact: address, hours, email, phone, embedded map, inquiry form.

Footer: About, Care, Warranty, Shipping & Returns, Privacy, Terms, social icons, newsletter signup.

Content and images

Auto-source high-res royalty-free product/lifestyle images from Unsplash (first choice), fallback to Vecteezy/Freepik; if paid allowed, prefer Shutterstock/Getty.

Hero 2400px+, product 1600px+, compressed WebP/AVIF with alt text.

Generate AI hero/category photos with reflective black backgrounds and bokeh, preserving jewelry realism.

E-commerce functionality

Full cart, checkout, and order flow with Stripe + Razorpay.

Guest checkout, email receipts, India tax/shipping rules.

Inventory tracking, SKUs, product variants (metal/size), wishlist, reviews with stars + count.

Coupons and automatic discounts with countdown timers for Offers page.

Performance and SEO

Mobile-first responsive.

Lazy-load images, next-gen formats, prefetch assets.

Core Web Vitals ≥ 90 on mobile.

On-page SEO: semantic headings, meta titles/descriptions, JSON-LD for Product, Breadcrumb, Organization, Offer.

Descriptive alt text, human-readable URLs (/collections/emerald-dreams, /product/emerald-cocktail-ring).

Sitemap.xml + robots.txt.

Accessibility

WCAG AA compliant: color contrast, focus outlines, skip links, aria labels, keyboard-navigable menus/carousels.

Analytics and marketing

GA4 + Meta Pixel with e-commerce events (view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase).

Newsletter integration.

Open Graph + Twitter Card metadata.
